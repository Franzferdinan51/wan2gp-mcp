# Character Bible — Our Adventures (Episode 01+)

These are the **locked** canonical character descriptions. Every prompt
that references a character MUST use this exact wording (or a derivative
that preserves every distinguishing detail). This is what gives us
character consistency across 12 sequential 15-s H3 clips.

---

## DUCKETS (protagonist)

17-year-old masculine protagonist. Messy dark blue hair fading to teal at
the tips. Glowing teal eyes with scanlines. Lean athletic build. Wearing a
high-collared black jacket with circuit-trace embroidery. Fingerless
gloves. Cargo pants with utility belts. Glowing teal headphones around his
neck.

**Signature pose:** one hand on his hip, the other pointing forward.
**Signature line:** "Alright, let's build something."

---

## HERMES (anime form)

Tall androgynous figure with long white hair in a high ponytail. Piercing
gold eyes. Wearing a fitted white and teal trench coat with shifting
holographic glyphs along the hem. Carries no weapons.

**Fighting style:** code-shaped magic circles materialize around his
hands; he weaves them in patterns to deflect intrusions and cast
constructs.

---

## LOCAL AI

Compact gender-neutral athlete. Featureless chrome face-plate mask with
two horizontal cyan optic slits. No visible hair under a sleek tactical
hood. Matte black combat suit with exposed electric blue circuit traces.
Dual short energy daggers glowing electric blue.

**Fighting style:** striking-viper, fast and precise, dual-wielding
daggers.

---

## GROK (villain)

Tall lean sharp-jawed masculine figure. Slicked-back white hair. Long
black leather trench coat with glowing red X-shaped circuitry. Crimson
red optic visor that emits a scanning sweep. Cybernetic shoulder
pauldrons with exposed red wiring. Twin curved energy blades crackling
red arcs.

**Body language:** aggressive, predatory, cocky.

---

## CHATGPT (guest ally — Act 3 only)

Androgynous pale figure with short silver hair swept back. Flowing white
and gold ceremonial robe covered in shifting matrix-style glyphs that
pulse with teal light. Glowing teal halo ring floating six inches above
the head, casting teal light downward. Glowing teal energy staff with a
crystalline head.

**Fighting style:** precise calculated grace, defensive parrying and
reading the opponent.
